# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity or expression, experience level, nationality, appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate, especially when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good faith, and ask for clarification before assuming bad intent.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people working in a second language.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, including about a person's
  identity
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately inflammatory remarks
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions or repeated unwanted contact
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions, mailing lists, chat, and events) and when someone is representing
the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers
privately. Reports are handled in confidence, and we
won't tolerate retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith. If your
concern involves a maintainer. 

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.
Depending on the severity, they may issue a warning, remove or edit comments,
temporarily or permanently ban someone from project spaces, or take other
action they consider appropriate. Maintainers will explain their decision to
the people involved.
