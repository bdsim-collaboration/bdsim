void BDSCollimatorTipJaw::Build ( )
overrideprotectedvirtual

Override function in BDSCollimator for totally different construction.

If the jaw is not built, do not take it's tilt into account for the vacuum box

The vacuum volume should extend from edge to edge, but the tilted jaws themselves don't Compute an effective length to correctly obtain the vacuum size at the edges

Rotate about y (from the z to the x axis) at x = 0 and translate The right jaw is at a negative half-gap
